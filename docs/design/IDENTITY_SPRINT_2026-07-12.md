## Class-lens skill system (weapons = verbs, class = language) (`lens`)

### Decision

Proposal 0 ("Class Lens as Data: Morph Cells + Grammar Registry + Sentinel Ledger Migration") wins and becomes the plan of record, amended with the degenerate judge's three CONFIRMED repairs (rule-slot remap for the frenzy→momentum merge, cap-clamped policy migration, formulaic arrival normalizer + automated blind screen for multiclass-imported cells) — all three verified by direct repo reads (comboRuntime.ts:60-77, rotationPresets.ts:283/286, MULTI_CLASS_PAIRS.md Layer 4). Two grafts survive from the runners-up: P1's id-frozen SignatureReplacement mechanism (cap ≤2 per cell, Pass 0 enforced) for mechanic-level lens moments the morph axes cannot express, and P2's Default-Weapon-Set Enforcement in full as its own wave (with its judges' berserker-signature, power-matched-grant, band-aware-fallback, and crafting-first repairs) — positioned deliberately AFTER the lens feel-slice waves and BEFORE non-native cells ship, because enforcement shrinks the reachable degenerate surface from 30 cells to the 15 Model-B defaults exactly when players would start optimizing into it. Wave 1 is a vertical slice the owner can feel in the live game: equip a dagger on a Witch Doctor (dev picker or live drop — access is not yet enforced) and watch dagger_stab feed soul_stack pips with Perfect@6 while the already-authored "Soul Reaping Strike" morphs render — one weapon, two languages, zero balance drift on native kits.

### Rationale

WHY P0 WON: highest judged scores (9 feasibility / 8 degenerate, no fatal flaws either lens), ~30 file:line claims verified by the judges plus my own spot-checks, and it is the only proposal that reuses the already-judged dual-wield sprint's steps 0-2 verbatim (staff hoist, @classCharge/@classWindow sentinels, cap-from-RESOLVED-spec, hunter requiresTargetTag), making the two locked plans one sequence instead of a collision. Its gate staging (byte-identical G-L0 before any balance change; exactly two measured re-gates; funnel kill-switch identity at intensity 1.0) is the most conservative of the three, and it answers GAME_VISION §5's triggering complaint (dagger funnel) with a measured dial plus spender/builder damage-share telemetry rather than vibes.

GRAFTS FROM P1 (feasibility 8 / degenerate 7): (a) SignatureReplacement — a full authored SkillDef variant that REUSES the base skill id (saves/bars/presets/scoped mods keep matching, zero migration), hard-capped at ≤2 per (class, default-weapon) cell by Pass 0; this is genuinely needed for Zombie-Dogs-vs-Arcane-Beam moments where a morph table can't change mechanics. (b) The auto-generated tooltip lens line ("spoken in Witch Doctor — chaos, poison, souls") and regenerating classes.ts resourceDescription strings from CLASS_LENS — single source of voice. (c) Verb-neutral authoring for NEW weapon kits from scythe onward (do NOT retro-verb-ify staff — P1's own judge showed that downgrades the live Asn×staff cell and violates its own default-cells-only rule).

WHAT THE JUDGES KILLED (and stays dead): P1's identity⊕profile split — its class-owned funnel/window schema cannot represent momentum (30/stack, Perfect ×3, opening icd 3) and frenzy (24/stack, +280%, arterial icd 4 + bleed detonate 40%) simultaneously, so its "re-parameterization not re-tuning" byte-identity target is mathematically unreachable and it contradicts the sprint's locked WD-dagger-caps-at-6 unit test. P1's "zero save migration by construction" — refuted by persisted rotationPolicy copies of the frenzy presets. P1's ailment-identity rule — DROPPED from this plan (not just deferred as content): the degenerate judge showed WD's poison rewrite destroys arterial's bleeding-gated window and Sor's elemental_cycle does the same; ailment identity re-enters only as per-cell authored content under the G-LENS gate, never as a global rewrite rule. P2's soft-penalty alternative (never proposed, correctly rejected), its greatsword-signature-with-no-kit contradiction, its iLvl-1 rusty-flail drop hole, and "trailing" crafting audit are all repaired in Wave 5 rather than inherited.

MANDATORY REPAIRS FOLDED INTO P0 (all verified myself): (1) LEDGER_FLAT_RULE/WINDOW_WET_RULE become array-valued per stateId (windowInert = ANY listed flat rule active) so momentum can carry both momentum.flatSpender and the remapped frenzy.rabid — the scythe brief's slot-conflict precedent, applied; E5/E5b re-runs join G-L1's gate list since both rules are reachable on one assassin build. (2) The frenzy→momentum save migration clamps stateCountAtLeast/stateCountBelow counts to min(count, resolved cap 5) — rename-only would leave persisted `frenzy >= 6/7` rules permanently unfirable (silent rot in an unattended rotation). (3) stacksPerGain gets a formulaic arrival normalizer (derived from builder cast cadence vs class cap so blind natural arrival stays below 80% of cap) plus a cheap automated blind-k̄/Perfect-exclusivity screen over ALL reachable cells — closing the multiclass-import degenerate (reroll pairs until an imported weapon's cadence lands blind arrival at cap = Perfect-every-cycle offline) at linear cost instead of hand-gating pairs. (4) Hunter's lens gains a data-first traveling mark source (castSkillRole-scoped builder-applies-mark innate) before any hunter non-bow cell gates — otherwise those cells have structurally zero window uptime. (5) The arterial window texture is pre-registered as G-L1's escape hatch (per-weapon window override slot) since the feasibility judge showed claws monetizes bleed via detonateDotPercent 40 and pure opening likely fails the ±5% total-damage assert — sanctioned, documented, class still owns the charge ledger.
